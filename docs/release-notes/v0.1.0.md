The first Juice Bar: the battery of your wireless mouse and keyboard, in the Mac menu bar and the Windows tray.

- Reads 2.4 GHz dongles, where the OS shows nothing: Kreo Swarm 75 keyboards and the Hawk Pro family mice (Amkette XS Flow Pro Plus too), plus any Bluetooth mouse or keyboard the OS reports
- A juice glass icon that fills to your lowest device: green above 85%, plain from 30 to 85%, amber from 20 to 30%, red below 20%
- Left click shows your devices and their %, right click shows Settings and Quit
- The keyboard updates the moment it wakes, from the dongle's own wake signal, with no polling in between
- Rename any device, hide the ones you do not want, and pick the menu text size (7 to 12 pt)
- Same look and feel as NetSpeedGauge, native on both systems, 0% CPU at idle

**Windows:** take `JuiceBar-x64.exe`, or `JuiceBar-arm64.exe` on a Windows on ARM PC, and run it. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the program is not code signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac:** take `JuiceBar-mac-arm64.zip`, unzip it and move the app to Applications. There is no version for Intel Macs. macOS blocks it the first time because the app is not signed by Apple. Open **System Settings**, **Privacy & Security**, choose **Open Anyway**, then open it again. Allow **Input Monitoring** when asked: the mouse dongle only answers with it on.
