Juice Bar 0.2.1: Bluetooth battery fixes.

- Bluetooth batteries are read once per connection, then Juice Bar only listens for the device's own updates. Some mice raise their Bluetooth battery value every time it is read, which made the % climb on its own; the % now matches what macOS shows
- When one mouse is on both its dongle and Bluetooth, Juice Bar shows the connection that has a real battery reading, so you always get a %
- A Bluetooth device shows right away while its first reading arrives, never as "Not connected"

**Mac:** take `JuiceBar-mac-arm64.zip`, unzip it and move the app to Applications (or let Juice Bar update itself: it asks first and checks our signature).

**Windows:** take `JuiceBar-x64.exe`, or `JuiceBar-arm64.exe` on a Windows on ARM PC.
